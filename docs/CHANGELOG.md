# Changelog

All notable changes to NH-Nodes. Versions follow the Comfy Registry releases.
"Behaviour change" marks fixes that can change the output of an existing workflow.

## 1.9.0 — Vision: body parts by name, Garment Segment on FASHN, parts in segs (2026-10-18)

One node is added, `Body Segment (NH)` (76 nodes in total). `Garment Segment (NH)`, `Portrait Segment (NH)`, `Segs Compose (NH)` and `Segs Select (NH)` change. Entries marked "behaviour change" can change what a saved workflow produces, so read them before you update. Workflows saved with 1.8.x still load and run: every new input is optional and every new output comes last. The licence notes at the end matter: `Garment Segment (NH)` is now documented as non-commercial, and `Body Segment (NH)` comes with use restrictions.

### Added
- `Body Segment (NH)` (NH-Nodes → Vision) masks the parts of the people in a picture that you name. Sapiens2 (Meta, 2026) labels every pixel of each person with one of 28 classes (face and neck, hair, torso, arms, hands, legs, feet, lips, teeth, tongue, glasses, upper and lower clothes, shoes, socks, accessories) or as background. It parses people like `Garment Segment (NH)` does: the whole picture first, then crops of the model's 3:4 shape around each person or group, parsed at 1024 × 768 (a person gets about 1024 px whatever the size of the photo), YuNet faces help to find people, and the work is done on a copy of at most 4096 px that is enlarged at the end.
- `Body Segment (NH)` reads `regions` in the formula language of `SAM3 Segment (NH)`: commas or new lines list several words, `+` and `|` join, `-` removes, `&` keeps the overlap, `~` inverts, parentheses group, and `background` (everything outside the people), `all`, `image` and `none` are keywords: `skin`, `hair, face`, `clothes - shoes`, `~person`, `skin - hands`. `and`, `or` and `not` are not operators here: they are refused with advice. The words are English (`skin`, `hair`, `face`, `arms`, `hands`, `legs`, `feet`, `torso`, `lips`, `teeth`, `tongue`, `mouth`, `glasses`, `clothes`, `top`, `bottom`, `shoes`, `socks`, `accessories`, `person`, with plurals, synonyms and `left` / `right` forms) or the Vietnamese words of the shared word list (`da`, `tóc`, `mặt`, `môi`, `răng`, `áo`, `quần`, `giày`…). In Vietnamese, `tay`, `đôi tay`, `hai tay` and `chân`, `đôi chân`, `hai chân` are the whole limb with the hand and the foot, `tay trái`, `tay phải`, `chân trái` and `chân phải` the whole limb of one side, and `cánh tay`, `bàn tay`, `bàn chân`, `bắp tay`, `cẳng tay`, `đùi`, `bắp chân` and `cẳng chân` the parts. Singular and plural are the same word (`lower leg` or `lower legs`). A word that has no region (`eyes`, `hat`, `bag`, `shoulders`, a wildcard, a Vietnamese word that is not in the list…) is refused with an error before the model loads, and the error says what to write instead.
- `Body Segment (NH)` returns `mask`, `cutout_rgba`, `mask_image`, `segs` and `report`. `segs` holds one instance per part found: hair, face, torso, arms, hands, legs, feet, lips, teeth, tongue, glasses, top, bottom, shoes, socks, accessories. `report` has one line per word with its share of the picture ("under 0.1%" for a tiny share, "of the pictures" for a batch), then notes where Sapiens2 differs from everyday use, and a line "no person found (none Sapiens2 was sure of, and no face)" for a picture with nobody in it (in a batch, "... in image(s) 2, 3"). The preview says it too. `tay - cánh tay` leaves the hands: the whole arm minus the arm alone. The node shows its result on itself (new optional input `show_preview`, on by default: the mask bright and the rest darkened, the parts it keeps outlined and labelled, and the report lines below; at most 16 pictures) and is an output node, so ComfyUI runs it even when nothing is connected to it.
- `Body Segment (NH)` has three model sizes in `model_size`: `0.4b` (the default; 1.6 GB download, about 1.5 to 2 GB of VRAM at the peak), `0.8b` (3.3 GB) and `1b` (5.9 GB). The weights are downloaded once from Hugging Face (`facebook/sapiens2-seg-<size>`, pinned revisions) into `models/NH-Nodes/sapiens2-seg-<size>/`, and only one size is kept in memory at a time. It needs PyTorch 2.4 or newer.
- `Body Segment (NH)` empties the map of a picture with nobody in it. When no face is found, two tests must pass: Sapiens2's first look must be sure (a confidence of 0.9 or more) of at least half of what it labels as people, and a second look at the mirrored picture must find the same people (the overlap, IoU, of the two looks must be at least 0.6). Otherwise the picture has nobody in it, and the report and the preview say "no person found (none Sapiens2 was sure of, and no face)". The second look costs one more look, only when no face is found. Before this, a facade or a café interior got faint "people", and everyday edits of a facade (exposure ×1.03 to ×1.15, contrast, saturation, sharpening) could lift the share of the first test above one half, which is why the second test was added. Measured with ComfyUI's decoder: people without a face overlapped 0.83 to 0.99 with their mirror (edited copies and small people on a plain background went down to 0.64), what the model invents on a facade 0.02 to 0.03. On 49 pictures without a face, 32 photos of people (backs, bodies without a head, a mannequin) were kept and 17 pictures of no one (a facade, a café, an elephant, flat lays of products, masks) were emptied. On 21 everyday edits each of the facade, the café and the elephant, the map was always empty. On 21 edits each of four faceless photos of people (a mannequin, a headless product shot, a dancer, a product on a body), it was always kept. A person whose face is found is never dropped.
- `Garment Segment (NH)` has a new optional input `model`: `fashn` (the default) or `fashionpedia`. `fashn` is FASHN Human Parser, a SegFormer-B4 for fashion with 18 classes (256 MB): clean tops, dresses, skirts, pants, bags, hats, scarves, glasses and jewelry. `fashionpedia` is the model of 1.8.0 and before, with shoes, socks, headbands and small parts (sleeves, collars, pockets).
- `Garment Segment (NH)` has new choices for `part`, next to `upper_garment`, `lower_garment`, `footwear`, `headwear` and `all_garments`: `dress`, `bag`, `belt`, `scarf`, `glasses` and `jewelry`. `jewelry` needs `fashn`: `fashionpedia` has no jewelry class, so the mask is empty and the console says why. With `fashn`, `footwear` is FASHN's "feet" class (the feet with whatever they wear, so bare feet too), `all_garments` is the upper, lower, footwear and headwear groups (bare feet included, as shoes were with Fashionpedia), `headwear` is the hat (there is no headband class) and `attach_parts` does nothing, because FASHN counts pockets, zips and patches as part of the garment. `attach_parts` works with `fashionpedia` only.
- `Garment Segment (NH)` and `Portrait Segment (NH)` have a new output, `segs` (NH_SEGS, last), with one instance per part found. Garment with `fashn`: face, hair, top, dress, skirt, pants, belt, bag, hat, scarf, glasses, arms, hands, legs, feet, torso, jewelry; with `fashionpedia`: its class names (shirt, top, sweater, …, sleeve, pocket, …). Portrait: skin, nose, glasses, eyes, eyebrows, ears, mouth, lips, hair, hat, earrings, necklace, neck, clothes. An instance covers its part on every person in the picture.
- `Segs Compose (NH)` has two new optional inputs, `segs_2` and `segs_3`: the instances of up to three nodes (`SAM3 Segment (NH)`, `Garment Segment (NH)`, `Portrait Segment (NH)`, `Body Segment (NH)`) of the same picture are mixed into one formula. For example, `bag` from SAM 3 in `segs`, the Garment segs in `segs_2` and the Body segs in `segs_3`, then `dress - bag - lips`.
- `Segs Compose (NH)` refuses segs of different pictures: another image count or size, or what looks like a different photo of the same size. Every frame of segs keeps a small grey print of its picture for this check. Edits of the same photo (brightness, contrast, colour, blur) are still accepted. The check is a guard against wiring mistakes, not a proof: it catches most other photos of the same size, a copy shifted or zoomed a little usually passes, and a new background behind the same person can fail. The new optional input `check_same_photo` (on by default) turns it off for edits like that. Segs made by older versions have no print and are mixed on size alone.
- `Segs Compose (NH)` and `Segs Select (NH)` understand each node's own words on that node's segs. Each node is read on its own: where a node has no instance carrying the word as its label, the word is read as one of that node's own words and takes the parts it stands for, and an instance of one node that carries the word does not hide another node's word (with the segs of `Portrait Segment (NH)` and `Body Segment (NH)` together, `skin` is the skin instance of the first and the skin parts of the second). Only group words stand for several parts. `Body Segment (NH)`: `skin`, `body skin`, `head`, `mouth`, `clothes`, `clothing`, `outfit`, `person`, `body`, and the exact other names `upper clothes`, `lower clothes`, `apparel` (`skin` is its face, torso, arms, hands, legs and feet parts, `clothes` its top, bottom, shoes, socks and accessories parts), their Vietnamese words (`da`, `quần áo`, `người`…), and, in `Segs Compose (NH)`, the whole limbs: `tay`, `đôi tay`, `hai tay` are the arms with the hands, `chân`, `đôi chân`, `hai chân` the legs with the feet, while `cánh tay` stays the arms alone (the side forms `tay trái`… and the pieces `left hand`, `thigh` find nothing there). `Garment Segment (NH)`: the groups of `part` (`upper_garment`, `lower_garment`, `footwear`, `headwear`, `all_garments`; with `fashn`, `upper_garment` is top, dress and scarf and `footwear` the feet). `Portrait Segment (NH)`: `face` (skin, nose, glasses, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, lips), `face skin` (the skin part) and `mouth interior`. A word that is the name of one of a node's parts never stands for other parts (Fashionpedia's `belt` is only its belt). The near synonyms of `Body Segment (NH)` (`dress`, `pants`, `skirt`, `jeans`, `shirt`, `neck`…) are not read: write `top` or `bottom`, or mix in the Garment segs, which label dress, skirt and pants. For example, `bag` from `SAM3 Segment (NH)` in `segs` and the Body segs in `segs_2`, then `skin - bag`.

### Changed
- (behaviour change) `Garment Segment (NH)` runs `fashn` by default. A saved workflow or API prompt without `model` (everything made before 1.9.0) now runs `fashn`, so its masks change. They are better: `all_garments` on an 8K group photo went from 7% to 33% of the picture, because Fashionpedia missed the dresses. `footwear` is now the feet (bare feet included) instead of shoes and socks, `headwear` has no headbands, and `attach_parts` does nothing. The first run downloads 256 MB, so a machine that is offline fails until the model is downloaded. To get the 1.8.0 result, set `model` to `fashionpedia`.
- (behaviour change) `Garment Segment (NH)`: a region shorter than 128 px is no longer parsed again close up, and the labels of the first look stay there. This removed the salt-and-pepper labels that the models gave on small, ambiguous things (a helmet held in the hand). `Body Segment (NH)` follows the same rule.
- (behaviour change) `Segs Compose (NH)` and `Segs Select (NH)` treat singular and plural as the same label: `arm` finds `arms`, `foot` finds `feet`, `shoe` finds `shoes`, `scarf` finds `scarves`, `calf` finds `calves`. A pattern now also finds a label that differs from it only by its plural (a SAM 3 concept written `people` is found by `person`). `glasses` (eyewear) and `glass` (a drinking glass) stay two labels. Wildcards are matched as written.
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)` asks SAM 3 once for a concept that a list names in singular and plural (`arm, arms`), and its report lists it once. `glass, glasses` stay two concepts.
- The console message of `Segs Compose (NH)` for a label that finds nothing also lists the labels that the segs hold.
- `Body Segment (NH)` refuses `beard` and `mustache` with "a beard is part of 'face' here, and no NH-Nodes parser has a beard class (SAM3 Segment can try 'beard')". Its report notes about the face class, a dress and a skirt appear only when the picture has some of that region.
- The `fill_holes` tooltip now differs per node: off for `Garment Segment (NH)` (an arm across the torso would be filled in), on for `Portrait Segment (NH)` (a face stays solid where glasses, earrings or hair cut into it).

## 1.8.0 — Vision: masks from one node, faces and clothes parsed close up (2026-10-18)

No node is added (75 in total). `SAM3 Segment (NH)`, `Segs Compose (NH)`, `Segs Select (NH)`, `Portrait Segment (NH)` and `Garment Segment (NH)` change. Entries marked "behaviour change" can change what a saved workflow produces, so read them before you update. Workflows saved with 1.7.x still load and run: the new input of `SAM3 Segment (NH)` is optional and its new output comes last.

### Added
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)` returns the mask of a formula, so one node gives "the person without the bag" or "the background". In `concepts`, commas or new lines list several things (their union, applied after everything else); `+` and `|` join, `-` removes (put spaces around it), `&` keeps the overlap, `~` is everything except, parentheses group, and `background` is everything outside all the instances that were found: `person - bag`, `hair, face`, `~person`, `background`. Several words in a row are one label (`red car`, no quotes), and `#` starts a comment where a word could start. Here `or`, `and` and `not` are plain words, so a phrase such as `black and white dog` reaches SAM 3 as written, and wildcards are refused (they work in Segs Select and Segs Compose). When a request uses `background` and does not name `person`, SAM 3 also looks for `person` on its own; those instances are part of `segs` and `count`.
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)` has a new output, `report` (STRING, last): for each concept the number found and the best score, the English a Vietnamese word became, a concept that was not found with the best score it reached under `threshold`, words that were not understood, and what `background` meant.
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)` shows its result on the node (new optional input `show_preview`, on by default): the mask bright and the rest darkened, the instances outlined and labelled, and the report lines below. The preview is at most 1536 px and shows up to 16 pictures of a batch.
- Common Vietnamese words work in `SAM3 Segment (NH)`, `Segs Compose (NH)` and `Segs Select (NH)`: people, body, clothes, accessories, nature, rooms, vehicles, food and animals. A thing followed by colours or sizes is reordered (`áo đỏ` is `red top`, `tóc dài` is `long hair`, `tóc vàng` is `blonde hair`). `nền`, `phông`, `phông nền` and `hậu cảnh` (also with a colour, as in `nền trắng`) mean `background`. `xanh` after a thing is ambiguous (blue or green) and is reported: write `xanh dương` or `xanh lá`. The labels of the instances are the English words. The word list is `core/vi_words.py`.
- `Segs Compose (NH)` reads the same formula language: commas and line breaks list several items, `background` (the same as `~all`) is new, several words in a row are one label (`red car`; quotes are still allowed and keep `all`, `image`, `none` and `background` as ordinary labels), `#` starts a comment, and `or`, `and`, `not` and the wildcards work as before. When `person hair` finds no label but `person` and `hair` both exist, the node stops and suggests `person + hair`.
- `Portrait Segment (NH)` and `Garment Segment (NH)` find people with YuNet, a face detector (about 230 KB, MIT licence, from OpenCV Zoo). It is downloaded once into `models/NH-Nodes/yunet` and runs on the CPU with the OpenCV that NH-Nodes already needs. If it cannot be loaded (offline on the first run, or an OpenCV that cannot run it), the console prints one warning and the nodes parse whole pictures.
- `Portrait Segment (NH)` parses each face on close-ups. The whole picture is parsed first (as a padded square, no longer squeezed). Faces are looked for on two sizes of the picture (at most 32; when none is found, the picture gets a grey margin and is searched once more, which finds a face cut by the frame), and each one is confirmed by the parser on a tight crop. A wide crop (hair, neck) and the tight crop (eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, only inside an ellipse around the face) then refine the whole-picture parse. A face that already fills most of the frame keeps the whole-picture parse. Half-body and group photos now get eyes, eyebrows, nose and lips, and group photos get every face.
- `Garment Segment (NH)` parses groups of people on crops. The whole picture is parsed first and kept. Groups of people (found from that parse and from faces) get crops of the model's shape (a row of crops for a wide group) that are parsed at the model's full resolution, and a crop grows when people run past its edge. A crop's labels replace the first look only where they are surer, away from the crop's edges, and only for parts on people already found, so nothing is invented on an empty backdrop.

### Changed
- (behaviour change) `SAM3 Segment (NH)`: a concept list that contains a keyword (`all`, `image`, `none`, `background`) or an operator character (`+ - & ~ ! | ( )`) now acts as a formula. Lists of plain words give the same result as before. `person, background` is the whole picture (a list is a union); `salt & pepper shaker` is the overlap of `salt` and `pepper shaker` (put it in quotes to keep one label); `person (adult)` is an error; in `car #2` the `#2` is a comment, so it finds `car`.
- (behaviour change) `Segs Compose (NH)`: a line break between two labels now lists them (it used to count as a space, which made it an error). Next to an operator a line break still joins, so formulas laid out over several lines keep working. Words in a row are one label: `person hair` used to be an error and is now a label (with the hint above when both parts exist), and `red car` needs no quotes.
- (behaviour change) `Portrait Segment (NH)` and `Garment Segment (NH)` masks change on photos where the subject does not fill the frame; they are better (see Added). The whole-picture parse of Portrait Segment, which is also the whole result when no face is found, is now a padded square instead of the picture squeezed into the model's square. A picture with a side longer than twice the model's 512 px is shrunk with area averaging before the model sees it, which is faster and keeps fine detail clean, so masks of big photos can differ slightly. On a photo longer than 4096 px the label map is worked out on a 4096 px copy and enlarged at the end (the models see 512 px at a time, so nothing is lost). Once faces are found, face parts that the whole-picture parse saw far from all of them (a reflection, a pattern) are dropped.
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)` is an output node: ComfyUI runs it even when nothing is connected to it. A copy you no longer need keeps running SAM 3 whenever its inputs change, until you mute or delete it.
- The `mask` of `SAM3 Segment (NH)` is the result of the formula. A plain list of words still gives the union of every instance. When nothing is found, the mask is empty for a plain list and the whole picture for `background` or `~person`.
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)` asks SAM 3 for candidates down to a score of 0.1 and applies `threshold` itself, so the report can say how close a missed concept came. The instances kept are the same as before: a score exactly at `threshold` is dropped, as SAM 3 does. Instances with exactly equal scores keep the order SAM 3 gave them (the sort used to be unspecified).

### Fixed
- (behaviour change) SAM 3 masks of things that run off the picture now reach the image edge. SAM 3 fades its masks along the border of the square view it works in (the last 3 of 1008 rows and columns), which left a ragged gap along the edge of a person cut by the frame. That band is now lifted where the object reaches it from inside, also in the close-ups of `balanced`. A thing that ends within the band (about 0.3% of the picture's side) above the edge is drawn down to the edge.
- (behaviour change) `Portrait Segment (NH)` found few or no eyes, eyebrows, nose and lips on half-body shots, and missed faces in group photos, because the model saw a small face squeezed into 512 px. `Garment Segment (NH)` had the same problem with small people. See Added.

### Notes
- ComfyUI's Unload Models button does not release SAM 3's VRAM while `keep_model_loaded` is on. Turn it off and run the node once, or restart ComfyUI.
- Known limit: the Fashionpedia model behind `Garment Segment (NH)` misses some tops, for example a blouse under a big hat.

### Planned
Not part of this release, and the names may change: a better garment model.

## 1.7.1 — SAM3 Segment with other SAM 3 packs installed (2026-10-18)

### Fixed
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)` failed with `FileNotFoundError: ...bpe_simple_vocab_16e6.txt.gz` when another pack's bundled copy of `sam3` was imported first. ComfyUI-RMBG puts its copy ahead of the installed package, and that copy looks for its tokenizer vocabulary one folder too high when it is not told where it is. ## 1.7.0 — SAM 3 segmentation and instance nodes (2026-10-18)

### Added
Six nodes under **NH-Nodes → Vision** (75 nodes in total) and a new data type, `NH_SEGS`. Nothing in the existing nodes changes.
- `SAM3 Segment (NH)`: finds every instance of the concepts you name (short English noun phrases, one per line or separated by commas) with Meta's SAM 3. Outputs the instances (`segs`), their union `mask` and the `count`; `Segs Preview (NH)` shows what it found. `detail_level` `balanced` (default) segments each instance a second time on a close-up cut from the full-resolution image, so small and medium objects get crisp edges even on 4K to 8K photos; `fast` keeps the whole-image pass only. Other inputs: `threshold`, `max_per_concept`, `checkpoint` and `keep_model_loaded`.
- `NH_SEGS`: for every image of a batch, a list of instances with a label, a score, a tight box and the mask inside that box only, so dozens of instances on an 8K photo stay light. Full-size masks are built only when a node outputs a `MASK`.
- `Segs Select (NH)`: keeps instances by label (`*` and `?` wildcards), score and size, sorts them (score, area, position, centre first, as found) and takes a slice, for example the second person from the left.
- `Segs Compose (NH)`: one mask from a formula over labels: `|` `+` `or` (union), `-` (minus), `&` `and` (overlap), `~` `!` `not` (everything except), parentheses, and `all`, `image`, `none`. `~person` is the background, `(wall | ceiling) - window` the walls without the windows. A hyphen inside a word belongs to the label (`t-shirt` needs no quotes), so put spaces around a minus: `person - hair`.
- `Segs To Masks (NH)`: one mask per instance, the union per image, the labels and the count. It stops with a message when the masks would need more than about half of the free RAM, for example hundreds of instances on an 8K photo.
- `Segs To Impact SEGS (NH)`: Impact Pack's `SEGS` for one image of the batch (`crop_factor` 3.0 like Impact Pack). Impact Pack is not needed for anything else in NH-Nodes.
- `Segs Preview (NH)`: draws the instances (tint, outline, label and score) on the picture they were found in.

### Notes
- SAM3 Segment needs an NVIDIA GPU with CUDA: the official sam3 code builds part of the model on the GPU, so there is no CPU mode.
- NH-Nodes does not install or download anything for it. You install Meta's `sam3` package yourself (the Vision guide gives the command, pinned to the commit NH-Nodes was tested with; newer commits with the same image API also work), and the weights are gated on Hugging Face: request access, accept Meta's SAM License and put `sam3.pt` in `models/sam3` (or a folder registered as `sam3` in `extra_model_paths.yaml`). The folder is shared with other SAM 3 packs, so an existing copy is found. The licence is in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- The model stays in system memory between runs and moves to the GPU only while the node runs, unless `keep_model_loaded` is on.
- `detail_level` has two levels for now, `balanced` and `fast`.
- SAM 3 describes whatever it looks at on a grid of about 288 cells. A subject that fills most of an 8K frame therefore gets edges at about 1/300 of its size: fine for the outline of a person, soft on hair, lace and fur. Hair-level edges are not part of this release.
- SAM 3 first sees the whole picture squeezed into a square, so on a picture wider or taller than 4:1 it can miss small things. The console says so; split such a picture into tiles with `Image Tile (NH)`.
- A file in the `sam3` folder that is not a SAM 3 checkpoint, or an incomplete download, is refused with the download advice before anything runs.

### Planned
Not part of this release, and the names may change: scene parsing that labels the whole picture (sky, wall, floor…), automatic concept tagging with ready presets and a report, matte refinement for hair and fur edges (it will add a `max` detail level), and parsing of each person's parts.

## 1.6.2 — Load images matching picks the right group

- `Load images matching` (behaviour change): when the source and the matching library share parent folders (for example
  `D:/Photos/Source/Nu/…` and `D:/Photos/Library/{Nam,Nu}/…`), the shared folders no longer outweigh the source's
  group, so a source in `Nu` picks `Nu/<match>` instead of the first folder in alphabetical order. Same-drive and
  cross-drive setups now rank identically, and folders elsewhere in the library no longer change which match wins.

## 1.6.1 — Cleanup and hardening

- Internal restructuring with no change to node ids, inputs or outputs: the large image-tools module is split into one
  module per node family, shared helpers live in `core/` (`image_folders`, `image_ops`, `mask_ops`, `model_files`), and
  the frontend extensions share one widget helper module.
- Widgets are hidden with the frontend's own `hidden` property when available (older frontends keep the previous
  method); values are still saved with the workflow.
- `Load Images Folder`: the image count used for auto increment now honours `recursive`; a wired `folder_path` no
  longer fingerprints ComfyUI's input folder by mistake.
- HTTP routes: the folder-count and large-preview routes no longer return absolute paths, answer with proper status
  codes (malformed JSON → 400) and do their file work off the server's event loop.
- `NH Save Large Image` accepts single-channel images; bfloat16 IMAGE tensors convert correctly; image conversions no
  longer use Pillow's deprecated `fromarray(mode=…)`.

## 1.6.0 — Performance, caching and VRAM

### Faster graphs
- **Lazy switches:** `If/Else (NH)`, `Switch N (NH)` and `Random Choice (NH)` now ask ComfyUI only for the branch they pick, so the other branches (for example a second sampler) are never executed. The choice itself is unchanged. A connected input whose upstream returns nothing still counts as connected.
- `Layer Stack Composite (NH)` is cached like any pure node instead of re-running on every queue.
- `Load images matching`: results are cached until the matched files change; the search stays inside the matching root (no more scanning parent folders, drive roots or the models folder). When nothing matches, `found` is `False` and `images` returns the new optional `fallback_images` input (evaluated only on a miss); without a fallback, `images` is blocked with a message and the rest of the workflow keeps running (behaviour change: it used to raise an error). Use `fallback_images` rather than routing `images` through a switch — a blocked input stops the switch too. A drive or network-share root cannot be searched recursively any more.
- Indexed loaders (`Load Diffusion Model Index`, `Load VAE Index`, `Load Clip Index`) reuse the model ComfyUI still holds, so editing a slot you did not select no longer reloads it from disk; they keep only a weak reference, so ComfyUI's RAM-pressure cache can still free the model (`--cache-classic` keeps a normal reference). VAEs are loaded with their file metadata and validated like the core loader does.
- `Mask Morphology (NH)`: rewritten on torch (CPU or GPU). Soft masks keep their gradient, masks touching the border no longer shrink from the edge, and large kernels are up to 100× faster. Even kernel lengths act as the next odd length (behaviour change).

### VRAM and models
- `VTON Ultimate Processor (NH)`: new optional `keep_model_loaded` (default **off**). Off frees the ONNX Runtime GPU memory after each run that really used the GPU (about 1.5 s slower per run); on keeps today's speed and is much faster for image lists, which ComfyUI runs one item at a time. ComfyUI is asked to make room before the models are loaded on the GPU, except once ONNX Runtime has fallen back to the CPU.
- SegFormer nodes now really make ComfyUI unload models when VRAM is tight (a device-index mismatch prevented it).
- Model downloads are pinned to tested Hugging Face revisions and only fetch the needed files; a half-downloaded VTON folder is repaired instead of trusted.
- `extra_model_paths.yaml` support: the folder names `nh_nodes` (SegFormer models, `.safetensors`) and `vton_mask` (VTON models, `.onnx`) can point to extra locations; existing installs keep working without moving anything.

### Viewer
- `NH Large Image Preview` / `NH Large Image Compare`: browse every image of a batch with ◀ n / N ▶ or the ←/→ keys.

## 1.5.0 — Correctness fixes

### Image
- **Batches everywhere:** `VTON Ultimate Processor`, `NH Simple Face Paste` and `NH Agnostic Image Generator` now process every image of a batch (smaller inputs with batch 1 are reused for every item). Behaviour change: Face Paste returns the full destination batch instead of one image. VTON stops with a clear error naming the batch index when no person is detected (it used to return a meaningless mask or crash).
- **Values are clipped before 8-bit conversion**, so overshoot from resampling no longer wraps bright pixels to black (Face Paste, Mask-Aware Resize, shared helpers).
- `NH Mask-Aware Resize`: an empty mask now respects the mode (pad = fit and pad with `pad_color_hex`, crop = cover and centre) instead of stretching.
- `NH Agnostic Image Generator`: masks of a different size are resized to the image, 2-D masks are accepted, soft masks keep their gradient, and the `noise` fill is deterministic (behaviour change).
- `Layer Layout Composite` / `Layer Stack Composite`: RGBA layers are pasted with their own alpha instead of as opaque rectangles. RGBA backgrounds give RGBA output.
- Padding colours accept `#RRGGBBAA`; without an alpha part the padding is opaque (it used to copy the blue value into alpha).
- `Save Image Path (NH)`: `filename_prefix` and `new_folder_name` can no longer write outside the chosen folder (on Windows a prefix may not contain `:`, which blocks drive prefixes such as `D:name`); a prefix such as `renders/img` creates the sub-folder. An absolute `folder_path` is still allowed.
- `NH Save Large Image`: WEBP (16383 px) and JPEG (65500 px) size limits are checked before anything is written; `--disable-metadata` is respected.
- `Image Label (NH)`: much faster font fitting (cached fonts + binary search), same sizes as before.
- `Image Grid Composite (NH)`: refuses a canvas that would not fit in the available memory with a clear message instead of crashing.
- `NH Large Preview Cache Manager`: actions run on every queue (it used to be cached).

### Resolution
- `NH Remove Resize Padding`: images resized with `cover_crop` are no longer stretched back (that distorted them). With `original_size` the picture is scaled back uniformly into the original frame and the cropped-away borders are filled from the edges; use `NH Restore Image Geometry` to composite onto the original instead.
- Resize nodes keep float precision for Lanczos/box/hamming (no 8-bit quantisation).
- `NH Smart Resolution Picker`: a stale preset or model name is reported as a warning in `info`.

### Logic, text and lists
- `Math Eval (NH)`: uses a shared safe evaluator. Behaviour change: an invalid expression now shows an error that names the expression instead of silently returning 0. `x / 0`, `x // 0` and `x % 0` give 0 with a warning. numpy/torch scalar inputs work.
- `Random Choice (NH)`: the weight list follows the input slots (`weights` item *i* belongs to `input_i`). Behaviour change when some slots are empty.
- Seeds are used literally in `Random Choice`, `Prompt Scheduler`, `Text Split Lines` and `Text Random Line`, so seed 0 is reproducible (behaviour change).
- `Prompt Template (NH)`: an empty variable no longer glues the neighbouring words together.
- `Regex Extract (NH)`: optional groups that did not match give an empty string instead of "no match".
- `Pack Universal (NH)`: values are stored by slot, so an empty `input_0` no longer shifts the other indices (behaviour change).
- `Compare (NH)` operator `in`: plain membership / substring test (no more fuzzy numeric matching).
- `Batch Merge (NH)`: RGB and RGBA batches can be merged; letterbox bars are transparent for both; very thin inputs no longer crash `pad`.
- `NH Mask Aspect Ratio Match`: `pad`/`crop` placement fixed (no crash at the image edge, requested size honoured) and the whole batch is processed.
- VTON garment masks: undetected keypoints and missing keypoint groups no longer stretch the mask to the image edges; small parsing islands are removed without cutting rectangles out of the garment.
- `Universal Slider Builder (NH)` is marked deprecated (it never produced output); existing workflows still load.
